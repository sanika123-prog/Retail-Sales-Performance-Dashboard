# 📊 Retail Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive **Tableau Retail Sales Performance Dashboard** designed to analyze sales trends, returns, customers, products, and country-level performance.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Retail Sales Performance Dashboard** provides a comprehensive view of retail business performance using interactive KPIs and visualizations.

The dashboard helps users understand sales performance, customer contribution, product revenue, return trends, and country-wise sales.

---

## 🎯 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard includes the following KPIs:

- 💰 **Total Sales** – Total revenue generated
- 🧾 **Total Orders** – Total number of distinct orders
- 👥 **Total Customers** – Total number of distinct customers
- 🔄 **Total Returns** – Total returned quantity

---

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Monthly Sales Trend
Shows how sales change month by month and helps identify sales growth and fluctuations over time.

### 2. Monthly Return
Displays monthly return quantities and helps identify periods with higher return activity.

### 3. Top 10 Products by Revenue
Highlights the top-performing products based on sales/revenue.

### 4. Top 10 Customers
Identifies customers contributing significantly to overall sales.

### 5. Sales by Country
Compares sales performance across different countries and identifies the strongest markets.

## 📊 KPI Calculations

| KPI | Calculation |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | `SUM(Sales)` |
| Total Orders | `COUNTD(Invoice No)` |
| Total Customers | `COUNTD(Customer ID)` |
| Total Returns | `SUM(Return quantity)` |
